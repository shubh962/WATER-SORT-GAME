This policy explains what information is handled when you play the Game on Android, and the choices you have.

1. Summary

  • The Game has no accounts and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • Your game progress (level, coins, settings, purchases you own) is stored on your device.
  • The Game shows ads served by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect certain device information to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.

2. Information handled by the Game

2.1 Stored on your device only

Level progress, coin balance, owned cosmetic items, settings (sound, haptics), daily reward streak, and records that a purchase was processed. This data stays on your device (and may be included in Android's device backup if you have backup turned on). We do not receive it.

2.2 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK. According to Google, the SDK automatically collects and shares the following for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

Consent. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states) the Game shows Google's consent form before requesting ads. You can review or change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy settings inside the Game (shown when required in your region).

Advertising ID. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (usually Settings > Privacy > Ads).

2.3 Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases (for example "Remove Ads", "PRO Pass" and coin packs) are processed by Google Play. We receive a confirmation that a purchase was completed (product ID and a purchase token). We do not receive or store your payment details.

2.4 Rating prompt and web links

The Game may ask you to rate it using Google Play's in-app review dialog, which is operated by Google. Links in the Game (privacy policy, terms, support) open in your browser or email app.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not run our own servers that store personal data about players.
  • We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or under the minimum age in your country).

4. Children

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Data retention and deletion

Because game data is stored on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the Game. Data that Google collects through ads is handled under Google's policies; you can manage it at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy and through your Android ad settings.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. We hold no personal data about you on our own servers, but you can contact us with any request at [SUPPORT EMAIL] and we will explain how to exercise your rights with Google. You can withdraw ad consent in Settings > Ad privacy settings (when shown) or in your Android settings.

7. International transfers

Google may process ad-related data in countries other than yours. Google describes its safeguards in its own privacy documentation.

8. Security

We keep the Game's local data on your device and use standard Android protections. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.
